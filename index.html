About

I am a Principal Engineer at WiSig Networks, where I lead the development of an O-RAN compliant radio unit that supports advanced receivers such as ULPI. I joined WiSig in 2018 as a Research Engineer, and over the years I have moved from research into product development, taking physical-layer ideas all the way into deployable radio products.

My research centres on the physical layer of 5G and the path to 6G. I work on low-PAPR waveforms and reference signals for the uplink, DFT-s-OFDM, coverage enhancement for large cells, and new waveforms such as OTFDM, which is targeted at IMT-2030. This work has resulted in journal and conference publications and a growing portfolio of US patents.

I completed my PhD at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad in 2026. I enjoy working where research and engineering meet, and I am always interested in conversations about uplink performance, waveform design and open, interoperable radio networks.
